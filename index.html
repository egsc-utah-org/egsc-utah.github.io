Mission

Supporting the people behind graduate engineering.

EGSC exists to advance graduate engineering education at the University of Utah. Its constitutional mission centers on a collaborative academic and research environment, strong graduate programs, and the success, well-being, scholarly growth, and professional development of engineering graduate students.

Build Community

Create spaces where graduate students can share concerns, find support, and work across departments.

Strengthen Programs

Bring student perspectives into conversations that shape graduate education and research culture.

Advance Success

Support scholarly, professional, and personal development for engineering graduate students.

Work

What EGSC is built to do.

The constitution establishes EGSC as a student-led organization with transparent governance, student representation, and responsible use of resources.

Represent Students

Program representatives and officers connect graduate student perspectives with the Executive Board and General Assembly.

Convene the Assembly

Members attend meetings, make motions, participate in debate, and vote on the business reserved to the Assembly.

Steward Funds

The Finance Committee and Board manage budgets, appropriations, reports, and reimbursements through adopted financial controls.

Grow Programs

Committees and volunteers support governance, development, student success, and other programs approved by the membership.

Governance

Student voice with nonprofit accountability.

01

General Assembly

The Assembly is the membership body. It elects and removes leaders, amends governing documents, establishes standing committees, adopts general appropriations, and serves as the final authority on constitutional interpretation.

02

Executive Board

The Board is the corporation's board of directors and manages organizational affairs except for powers reserved to the Assembly. It reports activities and organizational status at regular Assembly meetings.

03

Committees

Finance, Governance, Development, and other standing or special committees carry out focused work. Committee participation can include voting members and non-voting volunteers.

Participate

How graduate engineering students can take part.

Eligible graduate students become members by serving on a committee or the Board, or by attending at least one Assembly business meeting in the academic year. Members may attend, debate, make motions, and vote.

Attend

Join a regular Assembly meeting and bring questions, ideas, or proposals.

Volunteer

Contribute through committees, events, development, governance, or student support.

Represent

Work with your academic unit or graduate student advisory committee on Board seats.

Meetings

A predictable rhythm for public business.

Regular Assembly meetings are held in February, May, August, and November. The November meeting is the annual meeting. Board meetings are held monthly, and Assembly members may participate in person or remotely when an internet meeting service is provided.
